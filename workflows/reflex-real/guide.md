# Target: two-stage chain (Juggernaut XL, then Krea 2 Real)

Stage 1 builds the picture with an SDXL "DSLR" look; stage 2 gently re-renders it with a candid-photo LoRA, adding grain and imperfect, believable skin and light. The SAME prompt feeds both stages, so write one natural-language paragraph.

## How to write the prompt

- 45-90 words, English, flowing sentences. Start with shot type and subject ("A candid photo of ..."), then clothing, action, setting, light, framing, camera look (35mm, available light, slight grain).
- Realistic, slightly imperfect wording: natural skin texture, flyaway hairs, creased fabric. Avoid glossy words (flawless, perfect, cinematic, studio, airbrushed) and quality spam ("8k", "masterpiece").
- No weights or tag lists. No visible text (it will be garbled).
- Keep the composition simple and complete: the second stage preserves it but cannot repair a bad layout.
