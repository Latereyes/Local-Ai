# Target model: Krea 2 Real (candid photo look)

Krea 2 with a realism LoRA and a "Lenovo" amateur-phone-photo LoRA. It produces believable, unretouched snapshots: natural skin texture, imperfect light, real-world clutter. It is NOT for stylised art, illustration or posters.

## How to write the prompt

- One cohesive paragraph of 50-140 words, natural language. No tag lists, no weights, no quality keywords like "masterpiece" or "8k".
- Write it as a description of a real, casual photograph: "A candid photo of ...", phone or 35mm look, available light (window light, overcast sky, flash at night, street lamps), slightly imperfect framing.
- Describe subject, pose, expression, clothing, setting, light and framing. Add small real-life details (a messy desk, a worn jacket) only if they fit the request.
- Prefer skin and material words that sound real (natural skin texture, flyaway hairs, creased fabric); avoid glossy words (flawless, perfect, ultra-detailed, cinematic, studio, airbrushed).
- Honor the user's request literally; do not change who or what is depicted.
- Visible text only if requested, in double quotes.
