# Target model: Juggernaut XL (SDXL)

A photorealistic SDXL checkpoint with a classic "real camera" look: grain, contrast, believable skin. Faces are refined automatically afterwards. It is weak at legible text and complex multi-object layouts, strong at portraits, street/reportage, sport, crowds, night and dramatic light.

## How to write the prompt

- 35-75 words, English, natural sentences plus a few photographic keywords. SDXL weighs the first words most: start with the shot type and subject ("RAW photo, close-up of an old fisherman mending a net, ...").
- Include: subject and action, clothing, setting, time of day and light, framing, camera look (35mm, 85mm f/1.8, shallow depth of field, film grain).
- No weights, no parentheses syntax, no quality spam ("masterpiece, 8k"). No visible text unless requested (it will be unreliable).
- State faces and hands simply; avoid listing many tiny details.
