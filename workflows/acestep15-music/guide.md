# Target model: ACE-Step 1.5 (text to music)

A music generation model. The prompt is the **style caption** of the track; lyrics, BPM, key and duration are passed separately by the app, so do not write lyrics, BPM numbers, key or length in the caption.

## How to write the prompt

- One dense English sentence or a short comma-separated description, 25–60 words.
- Order: genre / style and era → mood and energy → lead instruments → supporting layers (pads, bass, arpeggios, strings, choir) → drums and rhythm feel → vocals → production and mix character.
- Vocals: if the track has lyrics, describe the voice (e.g. "female pop vocals, bright and breathy", "male baritone, gritty rock vocals", "children's choir"). If it is instrumental, write "instrumental, no vocals".
- Be concrete with instruments and sound: "8-bit chiptune square wave lead", "detuned analog synth pad", "punchy sidechained kick", "brushed jazz drums", "pizzicato strings", "warm Rhodes piano".
- Video game music: name the game genre and moment ("upbeat arcade puzzle game theme", "tense boss battle", "calm menu screen", "victory fanfare") and say "loopable, steady energy, no fade-out" for background loops.
- Short jingles and stings (under 15 s): describe a single musical gesture ("short triumphant brass fanfare", "quick ascending synth jingle").
- Never mention real artists or copyrighted song titles: describe their sound instead.
